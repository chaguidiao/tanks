<h1 class="nonum" style="page-break-before: always">About this guide</h1>

*Tanks* is a top-down, real-time tank command simulation. You command a company of tanks and fighting vehicles from a tactical map display. You give intent-level orders ("occupy this battle position facing north-east", "move to this checkpoint and wait for my code word"), and the crews and platoon leaders carry them out using real military procedures.

That is what makes the game different, and it can feel strange at first. You never drive a tank or aim a gun. You see only what your crews report, the reports arrive late, and your orders take time to reach the platoons over the radio. Winning comes from good plans, good positions and good timing, not from fast clicking.

### How this guide is organised

| Part | What it covers | Read it when |
|---|---|---|
| **I. Basics** | Starting the game, the screen, the map, selecting units, the basic orders, and a complete first battle step by step | First, before or during your first scenario |
| **II. Advanced** | Routes and checkpoint plans, code words, contingencies, the context menu, fire support, smoke, terrain analysis, how detection, gunnery and armour work, the database, the after-action review, the editors | Once you can win *Hold the Village* |
| **III. Strategy and doctrine** | The principles of tank combat, checklists for defence, attack and delay, era and weather notes, scenario hints, and a primer on the military doctrine the game models | Whenever a scenario goes badly, or out of curiosity |
| **Reference** | Glossary, keyboard and mouse reference, vehicle and ammunition tables, scenario list, troubleshooting | Any time |

### Conventions

- Keys are shown like <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>RMB</kbd>. **LMB**, **RMB** and **MMB** are the left, right and middle mouse buttons.
- Text exactly as it appears on screen is set in capitals, like **BATTLE POS** or `SET IN BP`. The game uses capitals everywhere, like a military display.
- Numbered yellow circles in the figures are callouts, explained in the table under each figure.
- Military terms are explained the first time they appear and collected in the **Glossary**. You don't need to learn them up front.
