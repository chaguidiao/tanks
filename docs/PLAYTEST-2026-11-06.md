# Playtest: newcomer run-through, version 0.1.3 (2026-11-06)

Played from a clean config, following `docs/guide/` as the only instructions, with real mouse and
keyboard input (xdotool on Xvfb, software GL, 1600 x 900). Screenshots named below are in
`out/playtest/shots/` (gitignored). No game code was changed.

| Scenario | How it went | Result |
|---|---|---|
| HOLD THE VILLAGE | Guide's first battle, step by step, including AAR and replay check | Won 06:10:48, no losses; replay hash matched |
| M1 - MOVEMENT | Routes, posture lists, halt/resume, reverse, break contact, plan, code word | Open-ended, all worked |
| FIRE SUPPORT | Call for fire, HE/smoke toggle, adjust, refused adjust | Mechanics fine, battle not finished |
| CLEARING THE VILLAGE | Move up, dismount, squads on foot under fire | Abandoned at 10:26, squads still walking |
| LONG DEFENCE | Battle positions, tailgate resupply, three waves | Won 06:23:28; 2 tanks and a truck destroyed, a third tank F-killed |
| HASTY ATTACK | Naive attack: straight at the objective | Won 08:36:21; 1 vehicle destroyed, others damaged |
| Editors, database, options, menus | Both editors, F9 play test, scenario save | Fine, see below |

Also: every scenario ran headless on seeds 1 and 2 with no crash (`tanks-headless run --summary`).

---

## High: gets in the way of playing

### H1. Crews keep firing at a target they cannot penetrate
In HOLD THE VILLAGE, 1-2 and 1-4 fired at immobilised tank 221 for about 139 s at 2.6-2.7 km. Of 58
Blue rounds, 35 went at that one tank, and almost all were `pen=false`. Other Red tanks closed in
meanwhile. The AAR line "HITS 51, KILLS 7" comes from this.

- Measured by replaying my saved battle event by event.
- Likely cause: penetration is deterministic (`p > armour / cos(obliquity)`), so a fixed geometry
  fails every time, and `choose_target` keeps the current target with no "no effect, shift fire"
  rule (`crates/sim/src/gunnery.rs:597`). 221 stopped at an angle.
- `tanks-headless duel` shows P(pen|hit) = 1.0 at 3 km for this pairing, so the duel harness does
  not reveal this. It may fire on a single face or aspect (not checked).
- It costs most where rounds are scarce, as in LONG DEFENCE.
- Suggestion: after N non-penetrating hits, prefer another target, and rank immobilised targets
  below mobile ones. Needs a doctrine citation and a golden hash update.

### H2. Move orders off the map are accepted, and platoons get stuck at the edge
Right-clicking the black area outside the map sends `MOVE TO GRID 000 044` and draws the X out in
the void. The platoon drives to the edge and stays `MOVING` indefinitely, speedometers running, and
the card shows `MOVING -0 M`. It also happens silently with group moves: keeping each platoon's
offset pushed platoons 2 and 3 off the south edge when I ordered all three onto the village.
Shots: `149-offmap-rmb`, `164`, `170`, `171-hasty-aar`.
Suggestion: refuse or clamp destinations at order time, including the offset ones.

### H3. The scenario objective is not shown on the game map
HASTY ATTACK says "seize the village" and the uplands map has at least five built-up patches. The
scenario editor draws the objective circle (`127-sce`), but it appeared in no game screenshot
(`148-hasty`). I had to read the coordinates from the scenario file to attack. Victory depends on
this area, including the "uncontested for 2 minutes" rule, so players need to see it.

### H4. The radio cannot be scrolled
The guide calls the radio "your main source of information" and says older messages scroll up, and
the log keeps 300 lines (`game.rs:419`), but only about 10 are visible and there is no scrolling.
In LONG DEFENCE a truck burned and I could not find out when. Also useful: click a radio line to
centre on its grid.

### H5. Input reaches the map through UI elements
- The wheel over any panel zooms the map (`33-radio-scroll`).
- Arrow keys pan the map while navigating a list or context menu.
- Left/Right in the AAR scrub the timeline and pan the map (`37-aar`, `43-after-aar`).

Cause: `MapScreen::camera` runs unconditionally (`mapscreen.rs:541`, `:623`).

## Medium

### Interface
- **M1. Your own units start hidden.** The map opens fitted to the window rather than the free
  area, so the south is behind the HUD. In M1 - MOVEMENT, the practice scenario, no unit of yours
  is visible at start; HASTY ATTACK is nearly as bad (`46-m1`, `145-hasty`). Open centred on your
  force, within the free area.
- **M2. Esc never deselects.** The guide says Esc steps back through pending click, tool, timing,
  then selection. With a selection and no tool it opens the screen menu instead (`159`). Cause:
  `esc_in_use` ignores selection and timing (`game.rs:409`).
- **M3. `KILLS` is not cumulative.** It went 7 → 0 → 2 in LONG DEFENCE (`116`, `118`, `119`). It
  counts destroyed contacts still in the picture (`game.rs:3131`). Make it a running count.
- **M4. The LOS height profile is drawn under the radio panel** and is almost unreadable
  (`160-los`). Placed at `game.rs:2046`.
- **M5. The recommended AUTO-PAUSE is unworkable in a big fight.** With CONTACT, LOSS, LONG DEFENCE
  took 10 resumes to get from 05:36:24 to 05:40:14 at X8, and it paused again straight after. Nothing says why the game paused. Resuming keeps X8.
  Suggestions: show the reason, debounce, and pause on the first contact of an engagement only.
- **M6. RESUPPLY and RECOVER are inconsistent with other orders.** The source list ignores number
  keys and shows no hints (`110-rlist`), though posture lists use them. RECOVER has no card button
  and is missing from F7 help, while the guide says every order is a button.
- **M7. The first guide step moves the map.** Step 2 says to click 1 PLT, which is already
  selected, so the map recentres. My BP 2 landed on the village as a result. Mention it in the
  guide, or don't select a platoon at start.

### Gameplay and balance
- **M8. Resupply serves by callsign, not need.** 1-1 went from 5 to 36 rounds while 1-4 sat at 1-2,
  and wave 2 then arrived. Serve the emptiest first, or share out evenly.
- **M9. An objective can be taken without a fight.** In HASTY ATTACK a plain road march with no
  smoke, artillery or recon won with 3 of 13 Red lost. The AAR shows about 9 Red vehicles intact
  roughly 650 m from the objective and no shots in the last 12 minutes (`171-hasty-aar`). It looks
  like the defenders displaced off the objective and then did not contest it. One run, so worth
  checking. The headless runs also ended in ObjectiveSeized (955-1106 s).
- **M10. The 1990 scenarios may not get harder.** The guide says each one removes an advantage, but
  autopilot Blue finished MIXED FORCE, FOG AT DAWN, NIGHT ATTACK and FIRE SUPPORT with 0 losses and
  7/9 kills on both seeds, the same as HOLD THE VILLAGE. Treat this as a prompt to check: Blue was
  AI-run, not a person. In my HOLD THE VILLAGE, Red fired 2 rounds.
- **M11. Foot speed against the time limit.** Squads made about 3 km/h at best, and two lagged at
  about 1.4 km/h early on, not moving "on line" (`100`, `102`). Dismounted about 1.9 km out, they
  would barely reach the village in 40 minutes. The guide could give a dismount distance.

### Scenario screen and text
- **M12. Newcomers are not steered to the tutorial.** The list opens on HASTY ATTACK, the tutorial
  is fifth, the years are not in order, and there are no difficulty or tutorial tags.
- **M13. Briefing errors.** `HOLD VILLAGE` appears for attack missions (`menu.rs:582`). Squads show
  as `UNKNOWN`, for example `RED · 4 UNKNOWN`, which also reveals Red's strength (`menu.rs:93`).
- **M14. Real vehicle names in player text.** Briefings and guide hints say M1, T-72A and BRADLEY
  while the game shows BULWARK and KURGAN. CLAUDE.md says vehicles use fictional names.
- **M15. The guide is out of date.**
  - It says version 0.1.1, October, two pre-generated maps (there are three), and "six scenarios".
  - First battle: it says contact around 06:10 from a column on the north road, 19 rounds, 16 hits,
    9 kills. I got 06:06 from the north-east and 58 rounds, 51 hits, 7 kills.
  - The GO/NO-GO text says light and dark shading; the game uses green, red hatching and amber with
    no legend.
  - The scenario editor key table omits `I` (squad) and `S` (supply point).

## Low
- The status card lists an out-of-service round: `APFSDS 0 · APFSDS 24` (`sim/src/lib.rs:252`).
  The two entries also share a name.
- `1 CASUALTIES` plural error (`game.rs:3782`).
- Menu title shows `HOLD THE VILLAGE?`: the truncation `…` is missing from the font (`menu.rs:425`).
- AAR `HITS BY RANGE` is cut off at the panel edge.
- FIRE SUPPORT status text overflows its box (`79-fire-run`).
- Labels overlap and become unreadable: carrier plus squad (`3322A 6/6`), stacked wrecks, LKP
  labels, and `1 (4)`/`2 (4)` at default zoom.
- The selection panel covers the north-east, where the enemy comes from in several scenarios, and
  grows with a draft. It ate one of my checkpoint clicks.
- Radio wraps early, leaving a lone `RED.` on the next line with room to spare.
- Break contact with no contacts still reports `THREAT GRID ...`, fires smoke, and leaves the platoon
  in COLUMN/TRAVEL.
- The radio said `2 EXPOSED` for tanks the roster shows as `MASKED`. These need different fixes,
  because a masked tank cannot see the threat at all.
- Battle position spread: the guide says tanks spread out, but platoon 2 formed two pairs about 50 m
  apart, against a doctrine spacing of 100 m (`25-card`). One position, so worth checking.
- The platoon reads `HALTED` after resupply instead of `SET IN BP`.
- With a group selected, only one roster row is highlighted.
- Plan draft starts from the old route, including a break-contact destination, with no clear way to
  start fresh.
- No heading tick on symbols, no observer marker on the viewshed, unexplained `T=0.00` on the LOS
  label, and `HE ROUNDS TYPE 72` in the editor.
- Key taps can be lost when the frame time exceeds the tap length. I saw this only under heavy CPU
  load with software GL, but old hardware is a stated target.
- Seen once, not reproduced: a right click on a checkpoint opened no menu.

## What worked well
- The core promise holds. Good battle positions and crews fighting alone are satisfying, and
  LONG DEFENCE was tense.
- The viewshed is excellent for planning, and "SET IN BP" came through at the time the guide says.
- Plans and code words worked first try: ETA 06:14 against 06:13:31 actual, with a clear `ALL SET`.
- Artillery radio flow and refusal messages are clear.
- Time compression held about 8x in a debug build on software GL.
- Replay reproduced exactly, there was no crash or panic, and unsaved-changes guards work.
- A suspended game survived trips through both editors.
- The in-game F7 help and tooltips are really useful.

## Not tested
- RECOVER (`O`): no tank was immobilised while a recovery vehicle was available.
- Tank smoke rounds, engine smoke, contingency firing, branches.
- Night and fog in the client (headless only), MARKET TOWN and the delay scenarios by hand.
- Terrain editor saving (avoided, to leave map data alone), GL 2.1 build, fullscreen, edge pan,
  Windows and macOS, and how it feels on a real GPU and mouse.
